A commercial license consists of:

1. an **Order Form** signed by both parties, which names the customer, the edition, the

   (template: [`legal/order-form.md`](https://github.com/envoscale/EnvoScale/blob/main/legal/order-form.md)); and
2. the **EnvoScale Commercial License Agreement** that the Order Form incorporates
   (template: [`legal/commercial-license-agreement.md`](https://github.com/envoscale/EnvoScale/blob/main/legal/commercial-license-agreement.md)).

Community License, [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.md).

one.
